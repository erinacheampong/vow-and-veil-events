Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

What services do you offer?

We offer full service planning, partial planning, wedding coordination, and day of coordination. Each service is tailored to your celebration, planning progress, and support needs.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend reaching out as soon as your wedding date and venue are confirmed. Full and partial planning clients often book 9 to 18 months in advance, while coordination services may be available closer to the wedding date.

Do you plan weddings outside Southern California?

Vow & Veil Events primarily serves Southern California. Travel may be considered for select celebrations based on location, timing, and event needs.

Will you be there on our wedding day?

Yes. Wedding coordination and planning packages include on-site support to oversee the timeline, communicate with vendors, manage key details, and help the celebration run smoothly.
